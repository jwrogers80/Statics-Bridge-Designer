Maximum load is the total load at which the first member reaches its capacity — the workbench's predicted failure load. Mass and load/weight cover the whole bridge: both plane trusses plus the cross members. Results scale linearly with load, so the ranking does not depend on the load the sweep is run at.

Configurations vary panel count × height. Only geometries that land on the 0.25 in grid are generated, so the K, Baltimore and Pennsylvania types — which need a joint at half height — use heights in 0.50 in steps. Option 1 is a coarse scan, good for ranking types; run option 2 on the winner to find its real optimum.
